Lesson 1: How to Write Korean Letters

Hangul is the Korean alphabet.

Korean has 14 basic consonants and 10 basic vowels.

Long ago, some Hangul vowels had little dots. Later, people changed the dots to short lines. This made Hangul easier to write.

How Korean Blocks Work

Korean letter blocks are made by putting consonants and vowels together.

consonant + vowel = syllable

ㄱ + ㅏ = 가 (ga)

ㄴ + ㅏ = 나 (na)

ㅁ + ㅏ = 마 (ma)

Each Korean block is like a little sound puzzle.
