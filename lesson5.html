Lesson 5: Learn 까, 따, 빠, 싸, 짜

In this lesson, we will learn five Korean double consonants and combine them with the vowel ㅏ.

Step 1: Meet the Double Consonants

These are the double consonants we will learn today.

ㄲ kk sound
ㄸ tt sound
ㅃ pp sound
ㅆ ss sound
ㅉ jj sound

Step 2: Add the Vowel ㅏ and READ the Syllables

The vowel ㅏ makes the sound ah.

double consonant + vowel = syllable

ㄲ + ㅏ = 까 (kka)

ㄸ + ㅏ = 따 (tta)

ㅃ + ㅏ = 빠 (ppa)

ㅆ + ㅏ = 싸 (ssa)

ㅉ + ㅏ = 짜 (jja)

Step 3: See how the Syllables are Used in Context

Look at the baby hippo and baby panda picture cards to help remember each syllable.

까

까다 = to peel

따

따다 = to pick

빠

빠 → 아빠 = dad

싸

싸다 = to wrap / cheap

짜

짜다 = salty

Step 4: Reading Practice

Read the tiny story. Try to say each sentence out loud.

아빠 와요. Dad comes.
귤 까요. We peel a tangerine.
사과 따요. We pick apples.
선물 싸요. We wrap a present.
짜요! It is salty!
아빠 웃어요. Dad smiles.
