What is a Dokkaebi? 도깨비란?

A dokkaebi (도깨비) is a magical being from Korean folktales.

Dokkaebi are playful, curious, and full of surprises. They love adventures, games, and helping others.

Dokkaebi carry a special magical bat called a 도깨비 방망이.

With their bats, they can create all kinds of magic.
