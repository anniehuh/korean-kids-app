Lesson 10: Review Vowels

In this lesson, we will review vowels from Lessons 7, 8, and 9 through two tiny Korean stories.

Words We Reviewed

These are the vowel words from Lessons 7, 8, and 9.

Lesson 7 Words

아가 = baby 야구 = baseball 엄마 = mom 여우 = fox 오이 = cucumber

Lesson 8 Words

요요 = yoyo 우유 = milk 유리 = glass 으악 = yikes 이모 = aunt

Lesson 9 Words

개 = dog 게 = crab 시계 = clock 과자 = snack 의자 = chair

Step 1: Reading Practice

Read the two tiny vowel review stories. Try to say each Korean sentence out loud.

Story 1

아가 와요. The baby comes.
이모 와요. Aunt comes.
요요 해요. We play yoyo.
우유 마셔요. We drink milk.

Story 2

개 와요. The dog comes.
게 봐요. We see a crab.
과자 먹어요. We eat a snack.
의자에 앉아요. We sit on a chair.
