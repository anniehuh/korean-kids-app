Lesson 7: Learn ㅏ, ㅑ, ㅓ, ㅕ, ㅗ

In this lesson, we will start learning Korean vowels. These vowels help us read Korean syllables.

Step 1: Meet the Vowels

These are the vowels we will learn today.

ㅏ ah sound
ㅑ yah sound
ㅓ uh / eo sound
ㅕ yuh / yeo sound
ㅗ oh sound

Step 2: Listen and Read the Vowels

Each Korean vowel has its own sound. Let’s read them slowly.

ㅏ sounds like “ah”

ㅑ sounds like “yah”

ㅓ sounds like “uh” or “eo”

ㅕ sounds like “yuh” or “yeo”

ㅗ sounds like “oh”

Step 4: Reading Practice

Read this tiny story using the vowels from Lesson 7.

아가 와요. The baby comes.
엄마 와요. Mom comes.
여우 봐요. We see a fox.
야구 해요. We play baseball.
오이 먹어요. We eat cucumber.
