✨ Meet the Dokkaebi Brothers ✨

Before you begin your adventures, meet Min and Jin, two kind dokkaebi brothers who grow their magic through learning.

What is a Dokkaebi?

A dokkaebi (도깨비) is a magical creature from Korean folklore. Dokkaebi are playful, curious, and full of surprises.

Unlike monsters or villains, dokkaebi are usually friendly. They enjoy games, challenges, and rewarding kindness and clever thinking.

Their magical bat, called a 도깨비 방망이, can create wonderful things.

🌟 Min (민)

Age: 4

Min is energetic, playful, and curious about everything. He loves robots, transformers, adventures, and following his big brother.

Min carries a small magical dokkaebi bat. His bat is still growing and learns new tricks every time he learns new words.

Favorite things: 🤖 Robots 🚂 Trains 🦊 Animals 🌈 Magic

🌟 Jin (진)

Age: 7

Jin is smart, patient, gentle, and kind-hearted. He enjoys solving puzzles and helping Min.

Jin carries a larger magical dokkaebi bat and often helps Min use his magic wisely.

Favorite things: 🧩 Rubik's Cubes 📚 Learning 🌟 Helping Others 🏆 Challenges
