Lesson 8: Learn ㅛ, ㅜ, ㅠ, ㅡ, ㅣ

In this lesson, we will learn five more Korean vowels. These vowels help us read more Korean words.

Step 1: Meet the Vowels

These are the vowels we will learn today.

ㅛ yo sound
ㅜ oo / u sound
ㅠ yu sound
ㅡ eu sound
ㅣ ee / i sound

Step 2: Listen and Read the Vowels

Each Korean vowel has its own sound. Let’s read them slowly.

ㅛ sounds like “yo”

ㅜ sounds like “oo” or “u”

ㅠ sounds like “yu”

ㅡ sounds like “eu”

ㅣ sounds like “ee” or “i”

Step 4: Reading Practice

Read this tiny story using the vowels from Lesson 8.

이모 와요. Aunt comes.
요요. Yoyo.
우유 마셔요. We drink milk.
유리 봐요. We see glass.
으악! Yikes!
