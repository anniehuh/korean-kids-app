Lesson 19: Learn ㄴ 받침

In this lesson, we will keep learning 받침. A 받침 is the final consonant at the bottom of a Korean syllable. Today we will practice words with ㄴ 받침.

Step 1: Meet ㄴ 받침

ㄴ can appear at the bottom of a syllable. When ㄴ is at the bottom, it is called ㄴ 받침. It sounds like a short ending n sound.

Look at these syllables with ㄴ 받침: 눈, 손, 산.

눈 nun
손 son
산 san

Step 2: Read Words with ㄴ 받침

The ㄴ 받침 sits at the bottom of the syllable. Each word below has a syllable with ㄴ 받침.

누 + ㄴ = 눈

소 + ㄴ = 손

사 + ㄴ = 산

눈 · 손 · 산 · 안경 · 사진

Step 4: Reading Practice

Read this tiny story using ㄴ 받침 words.

안경을 써요. We wear glasses.
신발을 신어요. We put on shoes.
손으로 문을 열어요. We open the door with a hand.
눈이 와서 우산을 써요. Snow falls, so we use an umbrella.
산 앞에서 사진을 찍어요. We take a photo in front of the mountain.
친구와 라면을 먹어요. We eat ramen with a friend.
