Lesson 24: Learn ㅈ, ㅊ, ㅋ 받침

In this lesson, we will keep learning 받침. A 받침 is the final consonant at the bottom of a Korean syllable. Today we will practice words with ㅈ, ㅊ, and ㅋ 받침.

Step 1: Meet ㅈ, ㅊ, ㅋ 받침

ㅈ, ㅊ, and ㅋ can appear at the bottom of a syllable. When they are at the bottom, they are called 받침. They make a short ending sound at the end of a syllable.

Look at these syllables with 받침: 낮, 젖, 꽃, 빛, 엌.

낮 nat
젖 jeot
꽃 kkot
빛 bit
엌 eok

Step 2: Read Words with ㅈ, ㅊ, ㅋ 받침

A 받침 sits at the bottom of the syllable. These words include syllables with ㅈ, ㅊ, or ㅋ 받침.

나 + ㅈ = 낮

저 + ㅈ = 젖

꼬 + ㅊ = 꽃

비 + ㅊ = 빛

어 + ㅋ = 엌

낮잠 · 젖소 · 꽃 · 불빛 · 부엌

Step 4: Reading Practice

Read this tiny story using 받침 words.

밤하늘에 별빛이 반짝여요. Starlight sparkles in the night sky.
아이와 고양이가 꽃길을 걸어요. A child and a cat walk on a flower path.
아이는 작은 장난감을 찾아요. The child looks for a small toy.
고양이는 반짝이는 나비를 쫓아요. The cat chases a sparkling butterfly.
아이가 “내 장난감이 맞아!” 하고 말해요. The child says, “It’s my toy! That’s right!”
별빛 아래에서 모두 즐겁게 집으로 가요. Under the starlight, everyone happily goes home.
