Lesson 20: Learn ㄷ 받침

In this lesson, we will keep learning 받침. A 받침 is the final consonant at the bottom of a Korean syllable. Today we will practice words with ㄷ 받침.

Step 1: Meet ㄷ 받침

ㄷ can appear at the bottom of a syllable. When ㄷ is at the bottom, it is called ㄷ 받침. It sounds like a short ending t sound.

Look at these syllables with ㄷ 받침: 걷, 닫, 듣.

걷 geot
닫 dat
듣 deut

Step 2: Read Words with ㄷ 받침

The ㄷ 받침 sits at the bottom of the syllable. Each word below has a syllable with ㄷ 받침.

거 + ㄷ = 걷

다 + ㄷ = 닫

드 + ㄷ = 듣

걷다 · 닫다 · 듣다 · 받침 · 숟가락

Step 4: Reading Practice

Read this tiny story using ㄷ 받침 words.

ㄷ 받침을 배워요. We learn ㄷ final consonant.
곧 문을 닫아요. Soon, we close the door.
숟가락 몇개? 손가락 몇개? How many spoons? How many fingers?
숟가락 5개, 발가락 10개 5 spoons, 10 toes.
선물 받아요 We receive a gift.
길을 물어요. We ask the way.
