Lesson 14: Learn 보, 소, 코, 토, 호

In this lesson, we will keep practicing Korean syllables made with the vowel ㅗ. The vowel ㅗ makes an “oh” sound.

Step 1: Meet the Sounds

These are the syllables we will learn today.

보 bo
소 so
코 ko
토 to
호 ho

Step 2: Add the Vowel ㅗ and Read the Syllables

The vowel ㅗ sounds like “oh.” When we combine consonants with ㅗ, we can read new syllables.

ㅂ + ㅗ = 보

ㅅ + ㅗ = 소

ㅋ + ㅗ = 코

ㅌ + ㅗ = 토

ㅎ + ㅗ = 호

Step 4: Reading Practice

Read this tiny story using words from Lesson 14.

호수에 보트가 있어요. There is a boat on the lake.
보트에서 소를 봐요. 소리가 나요. We see a cow from the boat. We hear a sound.
코끼리 코가 길어요. The elephant’s nose is long.
토끼가 토마토 먹어요. The rabbit eats a tomato.
호랑이가 호수에 와요. The tiger comes to the lake.
다 같이 봐요. Everyone looks together.
