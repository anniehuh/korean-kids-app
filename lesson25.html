Lesson 25: Learn ㅌ, ㅍ, ㅎ 받침

In this lesson, we will keep learning 받침. A 받침 is the final consonant at the bottom of a Korean syllable. Today we will practice words with ㅌ, ㅍ, and ㅎ 받침.

Step 1: Meet ㅌ, ㅍ, ㅎ 받침

ㅌ, ㅍ, and ㅎ can appear at the bottom of a syllable. When they are at the bottom, they are called 받침. They make a short ending sound at the end of a syllable.

Look at these syllables with 받침: 밭, 잎, 높, 좋, 맣.

밭 bat
잎 ip
높 nop
좋 jot
맣 mat

Step 2: Read Words with ㅌ, ㅍ, ㅎ 받침

A 받침 sits at the bottom of the syllable. These words include syllables with ㅌ, ㅍ, or ㅎ 받침. Some 받침 sounds can change when another syllable follows, but today we are mainly learning to recognize the 받침 letters.

바 + ㅌ = 밭

이 + ㅍ = 잎

노 + ㅍ = 높

조 + ㅎ = 좋

마 + ㅎ = 맣

밭 · 같다 · 잎 · 높다 · 좋다 · 까맣다

Step 4: Reading Practice

Read this tiny story using 받침 words.

아이가 밭에 가요. A child goes to the field.
밭에 초록 잎이 있어요. There is a green leaf in the field.
해가 높다. The sun is high.
하늘이 맑아요. The sky is clear.
아이는 “날씨가 좋다!” 해요. The child says, “The weather is good!”
큰 잎과 작은 잎이 같다. The big leaf and the small leaf are the same.
집에 가요. The child goes home.
밭의 끝에서 손을 흔들어요. The child waves at the end of the field.
