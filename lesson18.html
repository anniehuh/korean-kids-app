Lesson 18: Learn ㄱ 받침

In this lesson, we will start learning 받침. A 받침 is the final consonant at the bottom of a Korean syllable. Today we will practice words with ㄱ 받침.

Step 1: Meet ㄱ 받침

ㄱ can appear at the bottom of a syllable. When ㄱ is at the bottom, it is called ㄱ 받침. It sounds like a short ending k sound.

Look at these syllables with ㄱ 받침: 낙, 약, 국.

낙 nak
약 yak
국 guk

Step 2: Read Words with ㄱ 받침

The ㄱ 받침 sits at the bottom of the syllable. Each word below has a syllable with ㄱ 받침.

나 + ㄱ = 낙

야 + ㄱ = 약

구 + ㄱ = 국

낙타 · 막대기 · 박수 · 약국 · 학교

Step 4: Reading Practice

Read this tiny story using ㄱ 받침 words.

가족이 학교에 가요. The family goes to school.
학교 벽 색이 예뻐요. The school wall color is pretty.
식당 속 음식이 있어요. There is food inside the restaurant.
국을 먹어요. We eat soup.
약국에서 약을 사요. We buy medicine at the pharmacy.
박수로 시작해요. We start with clapping.
