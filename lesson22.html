Lesson 22: Learn ㅁ 받침

In this lesson, we will keep learning 받침. A 받침 is the final consonant at the bottom of a Korean syllable.
Today we will practice words with ㅁ 받침.

Step 1: Meet ㅁ 받침

When ㅁ is at the bottom, it is called ㅁ 받침. It sounds like the ending m sound.

Look at these syllables with ㅁ 받침: 김, 곰, 엄, 밤, 춤.

김 gim
곰 gom
엄 eom
밤 bam
춤 chum

Step 2: Read Words with ㅁ 받침

The ㅁ 받침 sits at the bottom of the syllable. Each word below has a syllable with ㅁ 받침.

기 + ㅁ = 김

고 + ㅁ = 곰

어 + ㅁ = 엄

바 + ㅁ = 밤

추 + ㅁ = 춤

김 + 밥 = 김밥

김밥 · 곰 · 엄마 · 밤 · 춤

Step 4: Reading Practice

Read this tiny story using ㅁ 받침 words.

봄이 오고 바람이 솔솔 불어요. Spring arrives and the wind blows gently.
아기 곰이 잠에서 깨어났어요. The baby bear woke up from sleep.
아기 곰은 힘이 넘쳐서 춤을 춰요. The baby bear is full of energy and dances.
엄마 곰이 웃으며 "우리 아기, 이름이 뭐지?" 하고 물었어요. Mother Bear smiled and asked, "My baby, what is your name?"
"나는 춤추는 곰이에요!" "I am the dancing bear!"
아기 곰은 몸을 흔들며 신나게 놀았어요. The baby bear wiggled its body and had lots of fun.
