Lesson 9: Learn ㅐ, ㅔ, ㅖ, ㅘ, ㅢ

In this lesson, we will learn more Korean vowels. Some Korean vowels are made by combining vowel shapes together.

Step 1: Meet the Vowels

These are the vowels we will learn today.

ㅐ ae sound
ㅔ e sound
ㅖ ye sound
ㅘ wa sound
ㅢ ui / eui sound

Step 2: Listen and Read the Vowels

Each Korean vowel has its own sound. Let’s read them slowly.

ㅐ sounds like “ae”

ㅔ sounds like “e”

ㅖ sounds like “ye”

ㅘ sounds like “wa”

ㅢ sounds like “ui” or “eui”

Step 3: See the Vowels in Words

Look at each picture card to see the vowel inside a Korean word.

Step 4: Reading Practice

Read this tiny story using the vowels from Lesson 9.

개 와요. The dog comes.
게 봐요. We see a crab.
시계 봐요. We see a clock.
과자 먹어요. We eat a snack.
의자에 앉아요. We sit on a chair.
