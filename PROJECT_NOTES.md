## The Dokkaebi Brothers

Min and Jin are two kind dokkaebi brothers.

They love adventures, learning new words, and helping others.

Their magic grows through learning.

Their bats grow stronger as they learn.

# Dokkaebi Rules

- They are brothers.
- They are kind dokkaebi.
- They may play harmless tricks on each other.
- They never use magic to hurt others.
- They help friends, animals, and magical creatures.
- Learning makes magic stronger.
- Kindness is more important than power.
- Creativity is more important than power.

---

# Shared Dokkaebi Powers

## Dokkaebi Lantern

All dokkaebi bats can become lanterns.

Command:

"방망이야, 길을 밝혀 줘!"

The bat glows and lights the way.

Uses:

- Find lost things
- Help animals
- Light dark paths
- Guide friends home

---

## Night Vision

Moonlight is bright to them.

They can see clearly at night.

---

## Forest Friend

Animals naturally trust them.

Examples:

- Rabbits
- Foxes
- Squirrels
- Fireflies
- Frogs

---

## Returning Bat Magic

A bat belongs to only one owner.

If someone else takes it:

- The bat tickles them
- They let go
- The bat flies back to its owner

---

## Tiny Magic

Young dokkaebi can create:

- Sparkles
- Bubbles
- Tiny rainbows
- Floating flowers
