What is Hangul (한글)?

Hangul (한글) is the Korean writing system. It was created in 1443 by King Sejong (세종대왕) and officially announced in 1446.

Hangul (한글) is one of the world’s most unique writing systems. It was scientifically designed, created as an original writing system rather than slowly evolving from older pictures or scripts, and it is successfully used today by an entire nation as its official daily language.

King Sejong (세종대왕) wanted ordinary people to read and write more easily. Hangul (한글) is special because its letters were designed in a logical and beautiful way.

How are Hangul (한글) letters made?

Many Hangul (한글) consonants show the shape of the mouth, tongue, teeth, and throat when making sounds.

ㄱback of tongue
ㄴtongue touches top
ㅁmouth shape
ㅅteeth shape
ㅇthroat shape

Vowels were inspired by simple ideas from nature:

ㆍsky/heaven
ㅡearth
ㅣperson

아 · 야 · 어 · 여 · 오 · 요 · 우 · 유 · 으 · 이

ㄱ + ㅏ = 가    |    ㅇ + ㅏ = 아    |    엄 + 마 = 엄마
