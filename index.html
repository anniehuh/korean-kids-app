Learn Korean with Hangul (한글)

What is Hangul (한글)?

Hangul (한글) is the Korean writing system. It was created in 1443 by King Sejong (세종대왕) and officially announced in 1446.

Before Hangul (한글), many ordinary Korean people could not easily learn to read and write because Chinese characters were difficult and required many years of study.

King Sejong (세종대왕) wanted everyone — including children and common people — to be able to read and write more easily. Because of this, Hangul (한글) is often considered one of the most scientific and logical writing systems in the world.

How are Hangul (한글) letters made?

Hangul (한글) consonants were designed to show the shape of the mouth, tongue, teeth, and throat when making sounds.

  • ㄱ shows the back of the tongue blocking the throat.
  • ㄴ shows the tongue touching the upper mouth.
  • ㅁ looks like the shape of the mouth.
  • ㅅ looks like the shape of the teeth.
  • ㅇ represents the shape of the throat.

Hangul (한글) vowels were also designed using ideas from nature:

  • • represented the sky or heaven.
  • ㅡ represented the earth.
  • ㅣ represented a person standing upright.

These basic symbols were combined to create vowels such as:

아   야   어   여   오   요   우   유   으   이

Hangul (한글) letters combine together into syllable blocks:

ㄱ + ㅏ = 가
ㅇ + ㅏ = 아
ㅇ + ㅓ + ㅁ + ㅏ = 엄마
